# N1 design quality gate

Review each relevant item as **Pass / Needs work / Not applicable / Not verified**, with an explanation and evidence.

## Product fit
- User job and audience are clear.
- The pattern serves a real task rather than showcasing AI for its own sake.
- The experience remains familiar and uses the right level of disclosure.

## Interaction
- Primary and secondary actions are distinguished.
- Loading, error, empty and successful states are handled.
- High-impact changes support confirmation, cancellation and recovery appropriately.
- Agent/model/context changes and persistence are visible.

## Content
- Terms match `voice-and-tone.md`, especially end-user "AI" naming.
- Error messages are actionable; success messages describe real outcomes.
- Permissions and data sharing are explained in plain language.

## Visual and responsive
- Approved component library and verified tokens were used, or deviations are explicitly provisional.
- Clear hierarchy and consistent spacing and affordances exist.
- Small-screen and input modality expectations are considered.

## Accessibility
- Contrast, focus, keyboard, screen reader, labels, state annunciation, touch and motion are checked.
- Manual testing requirements are identified; visual inspection is not called compliance proof.

## Handoff format
Return: task/purpose, evaluated persona, findings by severity, principle/rule, specific change, source confidence and follow-up validation.
