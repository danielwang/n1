# N1 audience and persona context

> **Context-based:** Names Sarah, Peter and Adam Admin appeared in previous N1 persona discussions. No validated persona research, demographic attributes or detailed persona documents are bundled. The roles and goals below are **task hypotheses**, not user-research findings.

## Sarah — chat user
- Likely job: Ask questions, use AI assistance for everyday tasks and continue conversations.
- Design considerations: Fast onboarding, clear assistant selection, legible responses, privacy cues and easy correction.
- Research questions: What are her most frequent tasks? Does she switch agents? Which devices and modalities does she prefer?

## Peter — chat user
- Likely job: Explore knowledge, work with project/course context, and retrieve useful outputs.
- Design considerations: Continuity, file/context awareness, reliable source cues, easy switching between tasks.
- Research questions: What distinguishes Peter's needs from Sarah's? When is multi-agent collaboration actually valuable?

## Adam Admin — administration plus chat
- Likely job: Configure organizational settings, manage AI capabilities and monitor operational issues, while also using chat.
- Design considerations: Keep admin and everyday chat contexts distinct; expose permissions, scope, confirmations and feedback.
- Research questions: What permissions and administrative responsibilities are established by role? What actions require audit trails?

## Additional educational stakeholders
Higher-ed use cases may include students, staff, educators and institutional admins. Do not equate these groups with the named personas without evidence.

## Applying personas
1. State the task and the actor's current permissions.
2. Identify critical success and failure paths.
3. Differentiate first-time, occasional and expert use only with evidence or labeled assumptions.
4. Avoid inventing quotes, motivations, demographics, usability metrics or validation results.
