# N1 voice and tone — working rules

## Core voice
Clear, calm, helpful, direct and respectful. Avoid unexplained jargon, exaggerated certainty and human-like claims about the assistant's inner state.

## Terminology
- Use **AI** rather than **N1** or **nebulaONE** for the assistant/system in end-user UI copy, based on an explicit prior terminology preference.
- Use **agent** for a configured agent when users must distinguish it from the general AI assistant.
- Keep feature labels consistent between navigation, settings and action confirmation.
- Use verbs that describe the result: "Save changes", "Remove agent", "Try again" rather than vague "Submit" or "Proceed".

## Tone by context
- **Onboarding:** welcoming, short, action-oriented; avoid overselling.
- **Chat:** conversational but concise; identify AI-generated material where users could confuse it with verified facts.
- **Warnings/permission changes:** unambiguous about scope, consequences and recovery.
- **Errors:** describe the issue, impact and next action; do not blame users.
- **Success:** confirm what changed, not just that something succeeded.
- **Admin:** more precise and explicit about responsibility, access and effects.
- **Voice:** short, speakable phrases; retain equivalent visual controls or transcripts where relevant.

## UX writing patterns
- Error: "We couldn't save your changes. Try again. Your previous settings are still in place." (Example only.)
- Confirmation: "Remove this agent from the conversation? You can add it again later." (Only when that recovery is true.)
- Pending: "Saving changes…" (Only when the interface is actually saving.)
- Empty: "No notifications yet." plus action guidance only if useful.

## Anti-patterns
Do not use vague AI anthropomorphism, blame, unexplained technical names, false success messages, or irreversible action labels that conceal the consequence.
